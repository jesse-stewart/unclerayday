About Uncle Ray Day

An unofficial holiday

Each December 23, we celebrate A Great Day in Central PA with Uncle Ray. This unofficial holiday commemorates how a single act of kindness can start a movement.

On December 23, 2021, Ray Kauffman braved winter weather and holiday traffic to pick up a friend in need and share an evening of camaraderie and fellowship.

This December 23rd, ask yourself this simple question:

What Would Uncle Ray Do?
